How Activation Works

One device, one MAC address, one playlist.

Every install of Emberplay reads a stable identifier from your device and uses it to activate your subscription — no accounts, no passwords to lose. Add your Xtream Codes details or an M3U link once, and your device stays connected to that playlist from then on.

1

Install & identify

Open Emberplay and it generates a device MAC address automatically — the same one your IPTV provider asks for when issuing a line.

2

Connect your playlist

Enter your Xtream Codes server URL, username and password, or paste an M3U link. Emberplay pulls channels, movies, series and the EPG in seconds.

3

Watch everywhere

Live TV, your on-demand library and a program guide are ready immediately, with favorites, parental PIN locks and continue-watching synced to that device.
